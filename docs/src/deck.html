Background: PicoRV32

THE CORE

  • PicoRV32 is a size-optimized RISC-V CPU core by Claire Xenia Wolf, released under the ISC license [1].
  • The core can be configured as RV32E, RV32I, RV32IC, RV32IM or RV32IMC [1].
  • On Xilinx 7-series devices, the core runs at 250–450 MHz in 750–2,000 LUTs, with a CPI of about 4 [1].
  • PicoRV32 is a general-purpose CPU, and RV32I is the 40-instruction RISC-V base integer ISA [2].

THIS BUILD: DEFAULT PARAMETERS

  • The default parameters are used: RV32I, with 32 registers and counters [1].
  • Multiply/divide, compressed instructions and interrupts are not included [1].
  • Misaligned accesses and illegal instructions cause a trap [1].

Process: the SkyWater SKY130 open 130 nm PDK is used, with sky130_fd_sc_hd cells [3].

Background on the core. PicoRV32 is a size-optimized RISC-V CPU core by Claire Xenia Wolf, released under the ISC license [1]. It can be configured as RV32E, RV32I, RV32IC, RV32IM or RV32IMC; on Xilinx 7-series devices it runs at 250 to 450 MHz in 750 to 2,000 LUTs, with a CPI of about 4 [1]. It is a general-purpose CPU, and RV32I is the 40-instruction RISC-V base integer ISA [2]. The default parameters are used in this build: RV32I, with 32 registers and counters. Multiply and divide, compressed instructions and interrupts are not included, and misaligned accesses and illegal instructions cause a trap [1]. The process is the SkyWater SKY130 open 130 nm PDK with sky130_fd_sc_hd cells [3]. On the limitations slide, "one 9-instruction program" means one short program, not one instruction and not one ISA. The full 40-instruction RV32I is implemented by the core, and 6 of those opcodes are exercised by the program. Formal equivalence checking covers all states and static timing analysis covers all paths. Sources: [1] github.com/YosysHQ/picorv32; [2] RISC-V ISA Manual Vol. I, RISC-V International; [3] github.com/google/skywater-pdk.

MODIFIED OPEN-SOURCE TOOLS

Where the open-source tools were changed

OpenROAD Resizer: repair

After routing, electrical and timing repair use the extracted parasitics, including zero-length shunt capacitances. The replaced estimate under-counted wire load 2.2 to 2.4 times on two measured nets. Driver sizing uses the corner model and the SDC slew limit.

OpenSTA in OpenROAD: parasitics

The SPEF reader and parasitic store give the Resizer the extracted RC network in a form its buffering model can use; constraint checks ignore empty exception entries.

OpenROAD PSM: static IR

Voltage sources can sit on each power pin's own routing layer as well as on the top layer.

LibreLane: flow integration

A post-route loop repairs electrical, setup and hold violations against fresh extraction, and the flow fails when a stated setup target is missed.

Yosys and ABC, Magic, KLayout, Netgen and Icarus Verilog were not modified. The changes are private and not published.

The guarantees of the method are described here, without implementation detail. One: the requirements were set once by the designer, and each decision was approved by the designer. The requirements are 0.25 ns uncertainty at ss, tt and ff, DRC clean in two independent decks, slew, capacitance and fanout at 0, gate-level simulation and LEC passing, and IR within budget. Four: every physical, DRV and simulation check was shown to fail on a known-bad input before it was trusted. Examples are gate-level simulation failing at 4 ns, the antenna check failing on a known-bad layout, the slew, capacitance and fanout check flagging a deliberately overloaded net with a 5 pF load, and the KLayout deck failing on a planted violation. Six: layouts built by Innovus are checked by Magic, KLayout and Netgen, and LEC links the routed netlist back to the RTL, so that the output of each tool is checked by a different tool.

HOW IT WAS FOUND

The fanout count was read back from the routed design and compared with the limit declared in the SDC. Signoff was refused at 240 pins over, although Innovus reported success.

17 · TRACE-V · PicoRV32 on SKY130

Defect one. A maximum fanout of 10 was declared in the SDC, and Innovus still finished with a 240-pin fanout violation while the flow completed normally. Nothing in the tool's own exit indicated the problem. The fanout count was read back from the routed design and compared with the limit declared in the SDC, and signoff was refused. The check has a control: a deliberately overloaded net, with a 5 pF load, must be flagged, otherwise the check is not trusted. The root cause is that the router's optimizer and the clock-tree builder each need fanout repair to be enabled explicitly. The two settings were found by agentic AI, and after the fix the violation count is 0. The consequence is visible in the clock tree: it grew from 23 to 174 buffers, and the insertion delay changed from 0.46 to 0.95 ns, as the average in the Innovus CTS report at ss. In the bars, the values before the fix are shown in gray and the values after in amber, each pair drawn to a common scale.

Defect 2: STA passed, simulation trapped

mem_ready → reg_op1

−1.87 ns

The interface was timed as the testbench drives it: memory is driven on the falling edge, which leaves half a cycle.

  1. After the fanout fix, setup was met by STA at 8 ns, but the post-layout simulation trapped at 8 ns.
  2. An instruction that had been given correctly was misread by the core.
  3. The cause was the absence of input and output delays in the SDC, so the memory interface was given a full cycle by STA instead of the testbench's half cycle.

HOW IT WAS FOUND

Equivalence of the open build: three setup steps

Yosys re-encodes state machines and leaves registers that the default configuration never uses. A plain key-point comparison against the RTL cannot prove equivalence. Conformal LEC 24.10 proves it after three steps.

STEP 1

Flip-flops renamed after their RTL registers.

The renaming script asserts that cells and connections are identical.

RESULT

Names change; cells and connections stay identical.

STEP 2

FSM encoding files for two state registers.

cpu_state: 8-bit one-hot to 7-bit one-hot. mem_wordsize: 2-bit binary to 3-bit one-hot.

CONTROL

Without these files, 1,246 points are non-equivalent.

STEP 3

Conformal setup analysis.

77 RTL registers were merged (duplicates and registers the default configuration never uses), and 5 were treated as constants.

RESULT

Equivalent: 1,959 points, 0 non-equivalent, aborted or unknown.

The Yosys netlist is also equivalent to the routed netlist of the final layout: 1,904 points, 0 non-equivalent.
